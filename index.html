Wenkai Fang方文凯方文凯Wenkai Fang

Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science计算机科学与技术 · 博士研究生

I am a Ph.D. candidate at Zhejiang University and a member of the Visual Intelligence and Pattern Analysis (VIPA) Group, advised by Prof. Mingli Song. I received my B.Eng. in Agricultural Engineering from Zhejiang University in 2024.

我是浙江大学计算机科学与技术学院博士研究生，隶属于视觉智能与模式分析（VIPA）研究组，导师为宋明黎教授。我于 2024 年获得浙江大学农业工程专业工学学士学位。

My research explores reinforcement learning for language model reasoning and general-purpose intelligent agents. I aim to build agents that move beyond benchmarks and make a positive difference in everyday life.

我的研究关注利用强化学习提升大语言模型的推理能力与通用智能体，致力于推动智能体走向真实场景，服务社会与日常生活。
